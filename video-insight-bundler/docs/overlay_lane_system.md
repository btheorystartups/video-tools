# Overlay Lane System

Overlay lanes group timeline overlays into speech/pacing, acoustic intensity, silence/pause, rhetorical pressure, topic continuity, visual reset, boundary diagnostics, recommendations, blockers, archetypes, and governance. High-severity and operational lanes are expanded by default; informational lanes remain collapsible.

The lane registry preserves access to every overlay while reducing timeline clutter. Lane summaries are review heuristics only and do not change governance status or edit media.
