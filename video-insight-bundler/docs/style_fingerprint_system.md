# Style Fingerprint System

Style fingerprints describe recurring editorial structure. They do not identify people, authors, channels, faces, voices, or real-world identity.

The fingerprint engines track:

- pacing signature
- continuity signature
- rhetorical density
- transition density
- callback density
- discourse density
- contradiction tendency
- ambiguity tendency
- visual continuity signature
- structural rhythm signature

Rhetorical, pacing, and discourse fingerprints are generated separately so reviewers can see which recurring patterns are structural, rhetorical, rhythmic, or discourse-state related.

The outputs are deterministic and local-first. They are intended to support human review and creator-level editorial improvement, not identity recognition or semantic truth.

Corpus fingerprints aggregate style, rhetorical, pacing, discourse, visual continuity, temporal rhythm, pressure, and recommendation fingerprints across multiple local videos. This remains editorial structure fingerprinting only.
