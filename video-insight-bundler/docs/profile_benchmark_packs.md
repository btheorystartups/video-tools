# Profile Benchmark Packs

Profile benchmark packs are deterministic editorial scaffolds for different video styles. They are not universal standards and are not forced when evidence is weak.

Initial packs include philosophical monologue, educational explainer, podcast conversation, documentary narration, keynote talk, tutorial instruction, cinematic storytelling, social short form, and interview format.

The temporal review system provides deterministic editorial-review guidance and timeline-linked evidence navigation. It does not apply edits, mutate media, infer identity, recognize faces, diagnose emotion, predict viewer behavior, or make autonomous creative decisions. Human review is required before production or editorial changes.
