# Comparative Intelligence System

Comparative intelligence supports deterministic creator-level and multi-video editorial analysis across one or more local analysis folders.

Generated artifacts live in `comparative/` and include:

- `multi_video_analysis.json`
- `style_fingerprint.json`
- `rhetorical_fingerprint.json`
- `pacing_fingerprint.json`
- `discourse_fingerprint.json`
- `creator_evolution.json`
- `structural_pattern_mining.json`
- `comparative_recommendations.json`
- `semantic_equivalence_scaffolding.json`
- `comparative_summary.json`

The comparison mode reads existing local artifacts only. It mines recurring structures, recurring recommendation families, pacing signatures, rhetorical signatures, discourse pressure signatures, creator evolution classes, and comparative volatility.

Creator evolution classifications are conservative: `stabilizing`, `converging`, `diverging`, `volatile`, `evolving`, or `inconclusive`.

Temporal, propagation, comparative, fingerprint, and semantic-equivalence-scaffolding outputs are deterministic editorial-analysis heuristics intended for explainability and human review. They are not semantic truth systems, identity-recognition systems, predictive systems, or autonomous editorial agents.
