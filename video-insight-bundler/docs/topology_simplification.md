# Topology Simplification

V69 classifies topology cycles and orphans before simplifying review navigation.

Generated files: `topology_simplification_report.json`, `cycle_reduction_report.json`, `orphan_reduction_report.json`, and `navigation_tree_report.json`.

Safe simplification does not delete audit evidence. It isolates hidden/internal, stale, and suspicious orphan nodes from main navigation and reports harmful cycle reduction evidence for review pathways.
