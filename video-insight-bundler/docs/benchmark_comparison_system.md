# Benchmark Comparison System

Phase 32 adds `dashboard/benchmark_comparison_registry.json` and `visualizations/benchmark_comparison_index.html`.

Benchmarks compare current video metrics against the local corpus distribution. They include percentile position, corpus median comparison, balanced/elevated/extreme classification, directional interpretation, and uncertainty notes.

Benchmark comparisons are local corpus review aids. They are not universal quality standards and do not claim causal certainty or viewer prediction accuracy.

The expert dashboard system provides deterministic editorial-review guidance and optimization interpretation. It does not apply edits, mutate media, infer identity, recognize faces, diagnose emotion, predict audience behavior, or make autonomous creative decisions. Human review is required before production or editorial changes.
